## Mini Guides

### Sanity Connect

1. Connect Sanity to Existing Project through Sanity Connect App
2. Choose Sync (Default Sync is fine for now)
3. Add `http://localhost:3000 `to your CORS Origin in your Sanity Project and **Allow Credentials.**
4. Create a new token. Viewer Role, call it Viewer. Get the token and it to your Hydrogen Storefront.
